# JanSeva AI - Government Welfare Terms & Acronym Glossary

This glossary clarifies common administrative abbreviations, welfare scheme terminology, and technical concepts used across the JanSeva AI Portal.

---

## 1. Scheme & Welfare Terminology

| Term / Abbreviation | Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer)** | Government mechanism to transfer financial subsidies directly into citizen bank accounts linked with Aadhaar. |
| **CSC (Common Service Center)** | Front-line delivery points for digital public services in rural and remote Indian villages. |
| **Seva Kendra** | Government facilitation office where citizens can apply for certificates and welfare schemes offline. |
| **BPL (Below Poverty Line)** | Economic benchmark used by Indian state governments to identify economically disadvantaged households. |
| **PM-KISAN** | Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi scheme providing financial assistance to landholding farmer families. |
| **PMAY (PM Awas Yojana)** | Flagship housing assistance scheme providing subvention for affordable housing construction. |
| **Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)** | National health insurance scheme offering coverage up to ₹5 Lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary care. |

## 2. Platform Technical Terms

| Term | Technical Explanation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)** | AI architecture combining TF-IDF scheme matching with dynamic contextual query resolution. |
| **Receipt Verification Hash** | SHA-256 digital signature printed on application receipts to prevent receipt tampering. |
| **RBAC (Role-Based Access Control)** | Authorization model separating Citizen, Seva Officer, and Super Admin portal permissions. |
