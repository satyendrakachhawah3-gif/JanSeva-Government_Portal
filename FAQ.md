# JanSeva AI - Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Welcome to the JanSeva AI Citizen Help Guide. Below are answers to common queries regarding government scheme discovery, application status tracking, document requirements, and security.

---

## 1. General Portal Questions

### Q1: What is JanSeva AI?
**A**: JanSeva AI is a unified public service portal that uses artificial intelligence (TF-IDF NLP algorithm) to match citizens with eligible Indian Central & State Government welfare schemes based on their profile (income, occupation, state, age, category).

### Q2: Is there any fee to apply for schemes on JanSeva AI?
**A**: No. JanSeva AI is a **100% free public service portal**. The government does not charge any application or facilitation fees.

---

## 2. Scheme Recommendations & Eligibility

### Q3: How does the AI scheme recommendation engine work?
**A**: When you input your demographic profile (state, income band, occupation, gender, age), our recommendation engine compares your profile against scheme criteria database and calculates a match percentage score.

### Q4: Can I search for schemes in regional languages?
**A**: Yes! The **JanSeva Chatbot Assistant** supports queries in both **Hindi** and **English** with text and voice input capabilities.

---

## 3. Applications & Tracking

### Q5: How do I track my submitted scheme application?
**A**: Go to the [Track Application](https://janseva.gov.in/track) page, enter your 12-digit Application Reference Number or scan the QR code printed on your application receipt.

### Q6: What documents are required for scheme application?
**A**: Most schemes require standard government identification:
1. Aadhaar Card (Identity proof)
2. Bank Passbook / IFSC (For Direct Benefit Transfer)
3. Income Certificate / Domicile Certificate (where applicable)

---

## 4. Security & Assistance

### Q7: How is my Aadhaar and personal data protected?
**A**: JanSeva AI enforces bank-grade encryption (TLS 1.3, AES-256 password hashing, masked Aadhaar storage). Your data is strictly used for official scheme verification and never shared with commercial third parties.

### Q8: Where can I find the nearest government facilitation center?
**A**: Use our **Find Nearest Kendra** interactive Leaflet map to locate Jan Seva Kendras, CSC centers, and government district offices in your pin code area.
