# JanSeva AI - Web Accessibility & WCAG 2.1 AA Compliance Policy

## Commitment to Accessibility
The JanSeva AI Government Portal is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for citizens of all abilities, including persons with visual, auditory, motor, or cognitive impairments.

---

## Technical Standards Implemented

| Accessibility Feature | Implementation Standard | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Screen Reader Support** | Semantic HTML5 tags (`<main>`, `<nav>`, `<header>`, `<article>`) & WAI-ARIA labels | Compliant |
| **Color Contrast** | Minimum 4.5:1 ratio for standard text and 3:1 for large text across light & dark themes | Compliant |
| **High Contrast Mode** | Dedicated high-contrast color scheme for visually impaired citizens | Compliant |
| **Keyboard Navigation** | Visible focus indicators (`ring-2 ring-blue-600`), Tab order, and focus trapping on modal dialogs | Compliant |
| **Voice Output** | Text-to-speech voice assistant synthesis for scheme explanations in Hindi & English | Compliant |

---

## Feedback & Accessibility Assistance
If you encounter accessibility barriers while using JanSeva AI, please contact our Accessibility Support Desk at `accessibility@janseva.gov.in`.
