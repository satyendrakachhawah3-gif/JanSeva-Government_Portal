# Contributing to JanSeva AI

Thank you for your interest in contributing to **JanSeva AI - Government Scheme & Citizen Service Portal**.

## Code Guidelines
1. **Architecture**: Maintain modularity across `server/services` (RAG & AI logic) and `client/src/pages`.
2. **Styling**: Adhere to the established Indian public service palette (`#0B2545` Navy, `#FF9933` Saffron, `#138808` Emerald).
3. **Commit Messages**: Use clean, descriptive human commit messages.

## Security & AI Safety
- Never store API keys or JWT secrets in tracked files.
- Always include AI disclaimer notices for synthesized eligibility outputs.
